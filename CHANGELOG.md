# Changelog

The version shows in Settings. Each release is a git tag (`v1.1.0`).

## 2.1.0

- Keepers are better and harder to beat from the start. They stay central to cover
  both corners, and the ball no longer passes through them.
- The ball moves faster on the pitch and in moments, and is bigger when you play it.
- A moment only opens when the ball has really reached you. No more teleporting.
- Offside now gives the ball to the other team, like every restart.
- Fewer stalls: corners are taken sooner, tackles do not leave the ball standing
  still, and play slows less when you have the ball.
- Defenders wide of the goal no longer float at the side of the shot view.

## 2.0.0

The 41-point request, finished and audited across both modes.

- Fixed: skipping a shot from halfway scored far too often. It now follows the same
  odds as playing it.
- The penalty scene shows the ball clearly, and its caption fits on a phone.
- Fixed from a full code review: Champions League clubs no longer play twice in a
  week, a flare-up that ends in bookings no longer sends the fouler off, free kicks
  survive a flare-up, midweek bans are the right length, a shot from far out always
  counts as one, and clinical finishing and Poacher now help on the shots you play.

## 1.23.0

- The club page has three tabs. My club: your standing, the coach, the dressing
  room, what is coming up and talking to the club. Squad: every player by line, and
  your place among them. Transfers: who is keen and every other club.

## 1.22.0

- The Champions League. Finish high enough and your club is in, with the format of
  the season: 36 clubs in one table from 2024/25, or 32 clubs in groups before.
  Then two-legged ties and a final.
- Win your league in a small nation and you get a qualifying tie for the last
  place, so even a club from San Marino can dream.
- European nights are midweek, so some weeks hold two matches. Fitness and rotation
  start to matter.
- It pays: money, fame, a new milestone, and it counts for the Ballon d'Or.

## 1.21.0

- The ability tree has its own screen: a big map to drag around, with a branch for
  each kind of player, plus physical, mind, set pieces, positions and weak foot.
  Your own branch comes first, and about a hundred nodes wait in it.
- Nodes cost level points and need an attribute. New techniques change the game:
  Poacher, Long ranger, Target man, Whipped crosses, Cool head, Coach's man, Iron
  man, Spot kick king, Ball winner, Reads the game and Commands the box.
- Learn as many positions as you like. Weak foot moved from the creator to the tree.
- Skill moves are trained now: pay the coach, wait a few weeks, one at a time. They
  no longer cost level points.
- Attributes give perks: physical makes training cost less energy, pace brings more
  fitness back each week, vision offers one more pass, composure keeps you out of
  trouble, and passing makes skill move training cheaper.

## 1.20.0

- Twelve formations: 4-1-4-1, 4-4-1-1, 3-4-3, 3-4-2-1, 5-3-2, 5-4-1, 4-3-1-2 and the
  diamond join the old four. Clubs now use different shapes.
- Coaches react at half time: chasing the game when behind, tightening up when well
  ahead or a man down. You are told when your coach changes shape.
- Managers can change shape at half time and from the touchline. The change lasts
  for that match.
- Play badly and the coach can take you off, around the hour. A goal or an assist
  keeps you on. It costs a little morale.

## 1.19.0

- Long shots get exponentially harder the further out you are. From 35 metres a new
  scene opens side on: read how far off his line the keeper is, choose to lob him or
  drive it, and hit a narrow window. From halfway it almost never goes in, unless
  he has wandered out.
- Tempers: players can square up. Most of it calms down, but it can end in cards,
  one man off, or both. If it is you, walk away or square up.

## 1.18.1

- Fixed: on an attack down the wing, keepers wandered far outside their posts.
  They now cover the near post.

## 1.18.0

- Set pieces have a short scene and then no clock on your decision. For a penalty
  the players clear the box. For a free kick the wall forms and the referee marks
  the distance. For a corner both teams crowd the box.
- Free kicks: shoot it or cross it, in your own time.
- Corners are yours to play. If you take them, pick your man and swing it in. If
  not, attack the ball, or wait on the edge of the box for it to drop.
- Headers are reworked. Read the ball's shadow and pick your run: near post,
  penalty spot or far post. Then one tap does the rest: when you tap times the
  jump, and where you tap aims it. A marker goes up with you.

## 1.17.0

- Goals put the scorer in the middle of the screen doing their celebration, in
  their kit. Your player has your look and your celebration.
- The referee is a figure now. He steps in for calls with the right signal:
  pointing to the spot, his arm up, a card in his hand, or waving play on.
- The referee shouts his calls: penalty, free kick, corner, offside and more. You
  can turn his voice off in Settings.

## 1.16.0

- The laws, for everyone. Cards come from real fouls, a second yellow is a red, and
  a red means a ban. Every side can win penalties, and players clear the box for
  them. If a keeper is sent off, a sub or an outfield player goes in goal. Too few
  players ends the match.
- Goals can be taken away: the flag goes up or the referee had already blown. You
  see the ball go in first.
- Advantage: the referee can wave play on. Some fouls are handball.
- The man called offside really is offside.
- Rules follow the season: VAR only in its own era, and three subs before 2022/23.

## 1.15.0

- Keepers have to reach the ball. A save only happens if the ball touches
  them: glove, fingertips or body, and you can see it.
- Keepers react late and move at real speed, and the better ones get further.
  Hard shots into the corners beat them; soft ones and shots near them do not.
- The ball now does what a ball does: held, parried back into play, tipped round
  the post or over the bar, in off a hand, off the post or the bar.
- Blocked shots can go behind, out for a throw, or stay alive.
- Rebounds are live: a parry or the woodwork can bring a second chance.

## 1.14.0

- Every person in a moment is now a jointed figure with elbows and knees, in
  this match's kits, and your own player has your look. No more circles.
- A keeper's dive bends and stretches instead of rotating as one piece.
- In pass picking, the team-mates you can choose stand up as figures on the
  pitch.

## 1.13.0

- Moments now show the real pitch: the keeper where he stands, the men who are
  actually in the way, your team-mates, in this match's kits. Play freezes while
  you decide and carries on from the same picture.
- Whether a shot counts as long range comes from where you really are.
- Defenders hold a compact shape, get goal-side and step into the line of the
  shot. The man who wins the ball is the man near it.
- The ball no longer jumps across the pitch, and keepers stay between their
  posts.

## 1.12.0

- The match clock runs about 1.8 times faster while play itself is slower
  and easier to follow. 1x is the new pace and stays the default. Quiet
  minutes fold away, and results are not affected.
- Commentary is readable: each line stays up for at least 1.6 seconds, and
  goals still cut straight in.
- Play match now opens the lineups, with each XI on a formation board. You can
  go back or kick off.
- Calls have clear jobs. Call for it makes your passes better. Make a run gets
  you more shots, and better ones.

## 1.11.0

- Fixed: tipped saves and deflected shots flew at the wrong goal, about one
  shot in six.
- The shot bar rises a little slower. The keeper rushes you faster.
- Training: Quick gives 1x. Play gives 1x, 2x or 3x for its stars. The base
  gain came down, so playing well matters more and careers still build up.
- Rest sits in the training grid. Energy reads out of 100. The Done for the
  week button is gone.
- Play: more room in the player card, and money and fame are just numbers
  now. Home moved into Settings as Quit to menu. The rating is the same size
  on Play and Train.

## 1.10.0

- World Cup qualifying for every nation: in the season before a World
  Cup, each confederation plays seeded groups of three or four, home and
  away, two matchdays at each international break. Group winners and the
  best runners-up fill the places, so even India can qualify. Your
  nation's group is on Tournaments and results come to the inbox.
- Tournaments follow their era: 32-team World Cups and 16-team Euros
  before 2026 and 2016, and the real calendar for the Copa America,
  AFCON, the Asian Cup and the Nations League.

## 1.9.0

- Life replaces More on the bottom bar, laid out as a store: This week,
  Shop, Cases and Kit tabs, items as square tiles, and a details panel
  that keeps its size.
- Play is calmer: money and fame sit in the player card, the week chips,
  rewards and season review are gone from it, and four shortcuts (Club,
  Tournaments, Awards, Leaderboard) sit under the match.
- Me is shorter: Settings is the gear at the top, rewards and the season
  review live here, attributes and This season are gone, and Growth is a
  compact card with a grid of facts under it.
- Talk to the club (raise, leave, loan), the league table and the
  dressing-room mood are on Club.
- Honours is a tab in Awards.
- Matches play at 1x by default; 2x and 4x are the faster speeds.
- New games have a season picker. Past seasons back to 2006/07 unlock as
  their squads are added.

## 1.8.0

- Life events with consequences: dressing-room rows, romance, nights out
  that end in arrests and bans (a third is prison), match-fixing offers
  that can come out later, charity galas, and rare career-ending
  injuries. A ban shows on the main screen.

## 1.7.0

- Life has This week, Shop and Cases tabs.
- Cases (Bronze, Silver, Gold) roll equipment from common to legendary:
  boots, gloves, shin pads, tops, headbands and watches that add to an
  attribute while worn. Wear the best, sell the rest.
- Cheaper shop perks: ice baths and a media coach.

## 1.6.0

- VAR: some goals are checked. Play holds while the referee goes to the
  pitch-side monitor; then the goal stands or is ruled out (offside, a
  foul, handball) and the defenders get a free kick.

## 1.5.0

- Stars dominate: team strength weighs great players more, and great
  shooters take and finish far more chances. A 95 side beats a 75 side
  almost every time; league scoring stays realistic.
- Stats grow about 50% faster from training and matches.
- At 4x and 8x, play no longer drops to slow motion when you have the
  ball.

## 1.4.0

- Beat your man and choose what comes next: shoot one on one, pass, or
  take on the next man. A second man is harder but pays more.

## 1.3.0

- Abilities is a skill tree per position: three branches of linked
  nodes, unlocked in order, with techniques at the end: Fake shot,
  Curler, Defence splitter, Last-ditch and Sweeper keeper, each changing
  a moment.

## 1.2.0

- Money and fame on the main screen: Life (money) and Clubs (fame) tiles.
- Clubs reworked: your club with its team rating and table place, then a
  search over every club with team rating, fame needed, agent fee and
  interest. Pitching to big clubs needs fame and costs an agent fee.
- Keen clubs get in touch through the inbox.
- More is grouped and simpler.

## 1.1.0

- Shooting: pick Driven, Curl or Low on every shot; a Pass button to
  play it to a mate instead; the shot starts from where you stand. The
  power bar is faster, MID and HIGH are narrower, and long shots need
  real shooting. Defenders the pitch has in the way are drawn in front of
  goal and block for real; the occasional full-stretch save.
- Passing: through balls and crosses open a short scene; a ball played
  well makes a bigger chance; assists pay more.
- Defending: a mistimed duel is punished with a better chance for them.
- Stats set the time you get in every moment game.
- The feed says exactly what the pitch shows: where each shot went, over
  or wide, blocked, tipped, and who cut out a pass.
- Moments open on the main screen in the pitch's place; the pass picker,
  skip choices and leave check sit under the pitch. Toasts at the top.
  Scoreboard with the clock and controls in the middle.
- Footballs everywhere, people in the moment games, goal celebrations
  with confetti; play at 1x is faster.
- Bench: skip to when you come on. Calls ordered Track back, Call for
  it, Make a run; Call for it gets you on the ball far more.
- Inbox: Important tab and varied media events. Rewards sit below the
  week's chips. Fixed: league table names, award tabs, search box focus
  outline, stray separators, wrapping labels.

## 1.0.0
